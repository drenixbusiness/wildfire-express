FAQs
straight answers.

Talk To Recruiting→
What CDL do I need to drive for Wild Fire Express?
A valid Class A Commercial Driver’s License is required for all OTR positions. We hire across the lower 48, and we accept CDLs from any state.
How much experience do you require?
A minimum of six months recent OTR experience in a Class A sleeper. If you’re close to that mark, talk to recruiting — we evaluate every application individually.
How often does Wild Fire pay drivers?
Settlements run weekly, Saturday-to-Saturday. Direct deposit hits your account the following Friday. No held-back weeks, no surprise reserves.
Do you offer fuel cards or sign-on bonuses?
Yes — fuel cards are issued on day one with a fleet discount averaging 30–50¢ per gallon. Sign-on, referral, and clean-inspection bonuses are seasonal; ask recruiting for the current offer.
Can I review my settlement before payment is processed?
Always. Each Saturday you receive your detailed statement to confirm. If anything looks off, accounting fixes it before the Friday payout — that is the whole point of the review window.
Where do I park my truck between loads?
Our main yard is centrally located in the U.S. with secure overnight parking. Drivers based out-of-region can use our partner network — no extra fee.
